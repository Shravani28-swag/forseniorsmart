Digital Payments

Learn UPI and QR codes step by step, and how to pay safely and confidently.

Lesson 1

What is a UPI app?

A UPI app (like Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm) lets you send and receive money straight from your bank account using your phone.

You link it once to your bank account and phone number — after that, paying is just a few taps.

Lesson 2

Scanning a QR code

Open your UPI app, tap "Scan QR", point your camera at the code on the shop counter, and it fills in the payee automatically.

Always check the shop's name appears correctly on your screen before you enter the amount.

Lesson 3

Confirming a payment

Type the amount, tap Pay, then enter your UPI PIN (a separate PIN just for payments) to complete it.

Your UPI PIN is different from your ATM PIN — never share either one with anyone.

Lesson 4

Checking transaction history

Open your UPI app and look for "History" or "Transactions" to see every payment you've sent or received.

If a payment fails, the money is usually refunded automatically within a few days — check history before resending.

Lesson 5

Getting help

If something goes wrong, use the "Help" or "Support" section inside your UPI app, or call your bank's official customer care number.

Get your bank's real customer care number from your passbook or bank card — not from a random Google search or SMS.
